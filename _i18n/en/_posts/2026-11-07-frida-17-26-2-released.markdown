Up to seven times the downlink through iOS device tunnels on Linux, thanks
to [@hsorbo][], and a fix for frida-server leaving *adb shell* hanging,
from [@tubibibo][].

## Idun

Håvard noticed that tunnel throughput on Linux was bound by main-loop
round-trips rather than CPU. On a slow host, downlink through a CoreDevice
tunnel sat at 70 to 90 Mbit/s with the kernel NCM driver or usbmuxd, and
around 30 Mbit/s with our own NCM driver, while the host was mostly idle.
Every transfer completion bounced through the main context and waited for
lwIP before being resubmitted, outgoing datagrams went through two idle
callbacks, each TCP read blocked until the lwIP thread had copied the data
out, and the tunnel pump waited for the netstack and an idle iteration
between reads, so decryption and lwIP processing never overlapped.

All of those round-trips are gone. Reads copy out under the state lock and
notify lwIP asynchronously, the pump reads back to back, and the NCM driver
parses and resubmits receive transfers on the libusb thread while outgoing
datagrams are submitted from the thread producing them. Teardown was made
explicit along the way: in-flight transfers are cancelled when the device
is closed, so it no longer depends on the device happening to send
something. On the same host, downlink went from 89 to 343 Mbit/s over
usbmuxd, from 73 to 311 with the kernel NCM driver, and from 32 to 242 with
ours. Uplink is unchanged, as writes still round-trip to the lwIP thread.
Thanks!

Håvard also found that *idund*, when spawned with stderr closed, as by
Node.js, ended up with its main loop's wakeup fd allocated as fd 2, which
detaching from the terminal then replaced with */dev/null*. The main loop
spun at 100% CPU polling it. The daemon now makes sure the standard
descriptors are open before anything else.

## Android

[@leonitousconforti][] reported that *adb shell* never returned after
frida-server crashed. Under *adb shell*, frida-server's stdin and stdout are
the same socket, and the crash monitor's logcat inherited it, so when
frida-server died abnormally, logcat kept the socket open and the shell
never saw EOF. tubibibo tracked it down and gave logcat */dev/null* for
stdin instead. Thanks, both!

Enjoy!

### Changelog

- idun: Keep the main loop out of the tunnel data path, up to seven times
  the downlink on Linux. Thanks [@hsorbo][]!
- idun: Ensure the daemon's stdio fds are open at startup, fixing a busy
  loop when spawned with stderr closed. Thanks [@hsorbo][]!
- idun: Skip the spawn test when the daemon path is unset.
- linux: Don't pass frida-server's stdin to the crash monitor's logcat,
  fixing *adb shell* hanging after a crash. Thanks [@tubibibo][] and
  [@leonitousconforti][]!
