A compiler release: a much more complete *fs* module for agents, thanks to
[@std-microblock][] and [@ChiChou][], two fixes for patterns compiled into
agents, and a hang on entrypoints with a colon in their name.

## frida-fs

Frida.Compiler bundles [frida-fs][], our implementation of Node.js' *fs*
module for GumJS, and this release moves it from 7.1.1 to 8.1.2. That
brings:

- *mkdir()*, *rename()*, *copyFile()*, *appendFile()*, *chmod()*,
  *chown()*, *utimes()*, *realpath()*, *symlink()*, *truncate()*,
  *access()* and *existsSync()*, contributed by [@std-microblock][].
- The *start*, *end* and *highWaterMark* options of *createReadStream()*,
  contributed by [@ChiChou][].
- A fix for read streams without an end yielding no data on Windows, where
  QuickJS evaluates *Infinity* to *NaN*.
- A fix for *readlink()* on procfs links such as */proc/self*, which
  failed with "invalid size". The buffer was sized from what *lstat()*
  reports, and procfs reports zero for its links.

Field reads also got cheaper, by passing offsets to pointer reads rather
than allocating a *NativePointer* per field. That means an agent using
*fs* built with this compiler needs Frida 17.20 or newer at runtime.

Thanks, both!

## Patterns

A pattern can describe both the on-disk and the in-memory form of a
format, and pick between them by whether *__MEMORY__* is defined. A
pattern compiled into an agent decodes live memory, so it is now defined
there, as pattern queries already assumed.

Addresses past 2^53, such as a kernel's, went through *parseInt()* on
their way into the compiled pattern and lost their low bits, so
*std::mem::base_address()* and *addressof()* were off by up to 2 KiB
there. They are now parsed as a *BigInt*, and only narrowed when that is
lossless.

## Entrypoints with a colon

Compiling an entrypoint whose name holds a colon hung. esbuild renames
such an output, so our bundler couldn't find it and panicked inside
esbuild's end-of-build callback, where the panic deadlocked on the very
build it was ending. We now tell esbuild what to name the output, and
report a failure there as an error.

Enjoy!

### Changelog

- compiler: Bump frida-fs to 8.1.2. Thanks [@std-microblock][] and
  [@ChiChou][]!
- compiler: Define *__MEMORY__* for patterns in agents.
- compiler: Keep wide pattern offsets exact.
- compiler: Name the entrypoint's output explicitly, fixing a hang on
  entrypoints with a colon in their name.
